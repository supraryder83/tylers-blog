Field Kit & Mobile Van Telemetry

The complete breakdown of Tyler Ziegler's high-tech physical security, RF analysis, and sub-zero locksmithing field kit.

DISPATCHED: Thursday, Oct 15, 2026
•
READ TIME: 3 min (594 words)

A mobile locksmith operating across rural Western Manitoba cannot simply run back to the shop when a tool fails or an unexpected cylinder appears. When you are 90 kilometers out on a snow-packed gravel road near the Saskatchewan boundary, your vehicle is your laboratory, machine shop, and lifeline.

Below is the verified inventory of gear deployed in our mobile unit and personal field pouch.

1. Non-Destructive Entry (NDE) & Pick Kit

Every technician knows that drilling a lock is the absolute last resort. Our primary kit is engineered for non-destructive manipulation:

⚡

NDE TOOL ROSTER // PRIMARY POUCH

TACTICAL PICK KIT
  • Bespoke Artisan Picks: Custom 301 High-Yield Stainless (0.019" and 0.015") with Manitoba walnut scales and brass ferrules.
  • Lishi 2-in-1 Decoders: SC1, SC4, KW1, KW5, M1/MS2, and Ford 8-cut HU101.
  • Disc Detainer Tool: Custom pick with rear-tensioning tip for Abloy Classic and Chinese disc padlocks.
  • Tubular Impressioning: 7-pin and 8-pin precision slip-collar tubular picks with calibrated friction bands.
  • Tension Arsenal: Top-of-Keyway (TOK) knurled prybars from 0.030" to 0.050", plus bottom spring tensioners.
  • Electric Pick Gun: Klom modified with custom balanced neodymium counterweight for micro-percussion raking.

2. RF, Wireless & Electronics Analysis

Modern physical security is inextricably linked with radio frequencies, contactless credentials, and encrypted communications.

⚡

RF & WIRELESS AUDITING SUITE

RADIO LAB
  • Software Defined Radio (SDR): HackRF One with PortaPack H2 (Mayhem Firmware) covering 1 MHz to 6 GHz. Used for auditing garage door frequencies, ag equipment wireless remotes, and ISM band telemetry.
  • RFID / NFC Toolkit: Proxmark3 RDV4 with standalone battery pack for cloning and debugging 125 kHz (Indala, EM4100, HID Prox) and 13.56 MHz (MIFARE Classic, Ultralight, iCLASS).
  • RF Spectrum Analyzer: TinySA Ultra (100 kHz to 5.3 GHz) for quick harmonic verification and spurious emission hunting.
  • Logic Analyzer: 8-Channel 24MHz USB logic analyzer for debugging SPI and I2C buses on electronic safe locks and gate controllers.

3. Sub-Zero Prairie Cold-Weather Gear

Tested down to -45°C across Westman winter blizzards:

  • Thermal Imaging: FLIR ONE Pro thermal camera (used to spot heat leakage around vault doors, verify electric strike power dissipation, and detect heated cylinder cores).
  • Flameless Pre-Heat: Milwaukee M18 Cordless Heat Gun with custom 6mm concentrated stainless nozzle.
  • Chemical De-Icers: 99.8% Anhydrous Isopropyl Alcohol pressurized flush bottle.
  • Winter Lubricants: Houdini Lock Lube, Super Lube Synthetic Dielectric Aerosol with Syncolon.
  • Emergency Power: NOCO Genius GB150 3000A 12V Lithium Jump Starter + onboard dual AGM battery isolator.

4. Key Generation & Bitting Measurement

⚡

KEY DUPLICATION & ORIGINATION

MACHINE SPECS
  • Mechanical Code Machine: HPC 1200 Blitz with carbide cutter wheels for standard commercial and residential keyways.
  • Electronic Originator: Silca Futura Edge with tablet touchscreen control for precision laser cuts, tubular keys, and automotive side-mill high-security keys.
  • Precision Metrology: Mitutoyo Absolute Digimatic 6" Stainless Caliper (calibrated to 0.0005" accuracy).
  • Impressioning Tools: Swiss No. 4 pippin files with optical magnifying loupes and diamond-treated clamping vises.

5. Mobile Van 4x4 Electrical & Living Architecture

Our 4x4 Ford Transit mobile workshop is self-sustaining for up to 5 days of remote field operations without external shore power:

  • 400Ah LiFePO4 Smart Battery Bank: Heated internal cells capable of charging in sub-zero ambient weather.
  • Victron Energy MultiPlus 2000W Inverter / Charger with pure sine wave output for sensitive test instruments.
  • 400W Monocrystalline Rooftop Solar Array.
  • Webasto 4kW Diesel Auxiliary Space Heater with direct fuel draw from the vehicle’s primary tank.
  • Starlink High-Performance Flat Mobile Terminal mounted on roof rack for uninterrupted prairie broadband.
