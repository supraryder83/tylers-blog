Every so often, you realize that the platforms we use to communicate on the modern internet have become louder, more transient, and increasingly disconnected from the simple joy of sharing things you care about.

This blog is an intentional step back toward something simpler. A personal space on the web that I own, without algorithms, infinite scrolling, or engagement metrics.

### What to Expect Here

I like building things. Sometimes that means physical workshop projects, small electronics, or custom jigs. Other times it means software, web tools, or simply reflecting on how we spend our time and energy.

Going forward, I will be using this space to document:

- **Builds & Projects:** Notes, photos, and post-mortems of things I am making.
- **Tools & Workflows:** The setups, software, and everyday equipment that actually hold up over time.
- **Occasional Thoughts:** Short notes on craft, learning new disciplines, and keeping things simple.

If you have stumbled upon this corner of the internet, welcome. Feel free to check out the [About](/about) page to learn a bit more about who I am.
