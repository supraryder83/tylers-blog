Hi, I'm **Tyler Ziegler**.

I live in Western Manitoba, Canada. I spend my time making things, tinkering with hardware and digital tools, and exploring the intersection of practical craftsmanship and technology.

### About This Blog

This site is my personal notebook on the open web. I built it to be clean, fast, and simple—running on [Grav CMS](https://getgrav.org), a modern flat-file content management system that keeps everything in plain markdown files without a database.

### What I Enjoy

- **Building & Prototyping:** Working in the shop with physical materials, CNC tools, and 3D printing.
- **Technology & Code:** Linux, open-source software, and practical automation tools.
- **Quiet Observations:** Exploring the prairie landscape, reading, and taking notes on how things work.
