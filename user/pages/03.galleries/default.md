# Curated Photographic Portfolios

A selection of ongoing visual series from Western Manitoba and beyond.

---

## Series I: Prairie Nocturnes & The Aurora

<div class="photo-feature-figure">
  <img src="/user/pages/images/prairie-aurora.jpg" alt="Prairie Aurora by Tyler Ziegler">
  <div class="photo-feature-caption">
    <span>PLATE 01 // Assiniboine Valley G4 Aurora Borealis • 21mm f/1.4</span>
    <span>WESTERN MANITOBA</span>
  </div>
</div>

The prairie night is not empty; it is a canvas of immense scale. Far from urban light domes, the southern skies reveal the arc of the Milky Way, while the northern horizon pulses with geomagnetic curtains. These long-exposure works study the interaction between immense celestial energy and the lonely, weathered architecture of the plains.

## Series II: Boreal Waters & Autumn Mist

<div class="photo-feature-figure">
  <img src="/user/pages/images/riding-mountain-mist.jpg" alt="Riding Mountain Mist by Tyler Ziegler">
  <div class="photo-feature-caption">
    <span>PLATE 02 // Clear Lake Dawn Fog & Tamarack Reflections • 45mm f/8.0</span>
    <span>RIDING MOUNTAIN NATIONAL PARK</span>
  </div>
</div>

Where the aspen parkland transitions into the Canadian Shield and boreal forest, water becomes a mirror. Shot from canoes at daybreak, this body of work explores mist as a sculpting element—isolating trees, softening distant hills, and rendering landscapes in tranquil, painterly tones.

---

## Series III: Monochromatic Heritage & Geometry

<div class="photo-feature-figure">
  <img src="/user/pages/images/monochrome-geometry.jpg" alt="Monochrome Architecture by Tyler Ziegler">
  <div class="photo-feature-caption">
    <span>PLATE 03 // Industrial Fire Escape & Shadow Steps • 35mm f/5.6</span>
    <span>PACIFIC AVENUE DISTRICT</span>
  </div>
</div>

A celebration of raw form, texture, and raking light. Utilizing dedicated monochrome sensors and optical glass contrast filters, these images strip away color distractions to reveal the structural rhythm of century-old industrial brick, weathered steel, and diagonal morning shadows.

## Series IV: Analog Craft & Optical Mechanics

<div class="photo-feature-figure">
  <img src="/user/pages/images/rangefinder-gear.jpg" alt="Rangefinder Camera by Tyler Ziegler">
  <div class="photo-feature-caption">
    <span>PLATE 04 // The Rangefinder & Silver Emulsion • 50mm f/2.0</span>
    <span>WORKSHOP BENCH</span>
  </div>
</div>

A photographic examination of the tools of the craft itself: mechanical brass camera bodies, precision helicoids, glass coatings, and the patient physical alchemy of gelatin silver film processing in the darkroom.
