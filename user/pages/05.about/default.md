# About Tyler Ziegler

I am a landscape, analog rangefinder, and architectural photographer based on the plains of **Western Manitoba, Canada**.

My visual work focuses on the geometry of light, atmospheric stillness, and the interplay between human craft and the natural world. Growing up and living in rural Manitoba has cultivated an appreciation for negative space, harsh seasons, and the profound silence of the prairie night.

---

## Photographic Philosophy

1. **Craft Over Convenience:** I value manual, deliberate tools—rangefinders, mechanical shutters, prime optics, and analog film emulsions. Stripping away automated computational algorithms brings a direct physical connection to the moment of exposure.
2. **Sub-Zero Endurance:** Some of the most extraordinary atmospheric light in the world occurs between -20°C and -40°C on the Canadian prairies: ice crystal halos, light pillars, and high-latitude geomagnetic storms. Embracing the cold is essential to this work.
3. **Print as the Final Medium:** A photograph is not truly complete until it exists as physical ink on archival cotton rag paper. The tactile texture of Hahnemühle Photo Rag and traditional silver gelatin prints anchor digital captures back into the physical realm.
