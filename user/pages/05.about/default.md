# About Tyler Ziegler

I am a machinist, digital fabricator, and hardware maker based in **Western Manitoba, Canada**.

My work focuses on the intersection of computer-aided manufacturing (CAM), precision CNC machining, industrial laser optics, and custom shop instrumentation. From developing optimized chip-thinning toolpaths in aluminum to tuning MOPA fiber laser pulse durations for sub-micron surface texturing, I build physical hardware designed for performance and reliability.

---

## 🛠️ Workshop Philosophy

1. **Understand the Physics First:** Whether it is thermal dissipation in a milling cutter or Rayleigh range in a laser focal beam, reliable fabrication starts with mechanics and material science rather than trial-and-error guesswork.
2. **Custom Tooling is Force Multiplication:** The best investment in any machine shop is fixturing and tooling: vacuum fixture plates, custom CoreXY soft jaws, automated air-blast lubrication, and calibrated probe routines.
3. **Open-Source Hardware & Code:** I believe in sharing repeatable parameters, CAD/CAM templates, and macro scripts. Every build log includes concrete numbers: feed rates, pulse frequencies, gas pressures, and G-code excerpts.

---

## ⚙️ Shop Capabilities

- **Subtractive Machining:** 3-Axis CNC high-rigidity milling in 6061-T6 aluminum, brass, acetal/Delrin, and carbon fiber plate.
- **Industrial Lasers:** 50W MOPA fiber laser (deep engraving, titanium color annealing, rotary 360° chuck marking) & 130W Reci CO2 laser (precision vector cutting & flame-polished acrylic).
- **Additive Manufacturing:** Enclosed high-temp CoreXY 3D printing in carbon-fiber nylon (PA6-CF / PA12-CF), PC, and engineering thermoplastics.
- **CAD/CAM & Controls:** Autodesk Fusion 360, LightBurn DSP, Klipper, LinuxCNC, and custom Python G-code post-processors.
