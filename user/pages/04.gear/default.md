## 🔍 Optical Glass & Prime Lenses

- **Voigtländer 21mm f/1.4 Nokton Aspherical:** My primary nocturne and aurora lens. Astigmatism and coma are exceptionally controlled at f/1.4, allowing pinpoint stars edge-to-edge.
- **Leica Summicron-M 35mm f/2 ASPH:** The everyday documentary standard. Incredible micro-contrast, zero distortion, and ultra-compact brass mechanics.
- **Leitz Summilux 50mm f/1.4 (Pre-ASPH):** Classic optical formula delivering a dreamlike, creamy rendering wide open that snaps into biting resolution by f/2.8.
- **Fujinon GF 45-100mm f/4 R LM OIS WR:** Landscape workhorse on the medium format GFX system. Weather-sealed against blowing prairie snow and rain.

## 🧪 Darkroom Chemistry & Film Scanning

- **Film Stocks:** Kodak Tri-X 400 (for grit and contrast), Ilford HP5+ (pushed to 1600 in winter), and Kodak Portra 400.
- **Developers:** Kodak D-76 (1:1 dilution) for classic tonal gradations; Rodinal (1:50) for acutance and edge sharpness.
- **Digitization Bench:** Custom macro camera scanning rig utilizing a Sony A7R V, 90mm f/2.8 Macro lens, and a 99 CRI bi-color LED light panel with Negative Lab Pro in Lightroom.
