### The Architecture of Mechanical Vision

Modern mirrorless cameras with artificial intelligence autofocus and high-frequency electronic viewfinders are miraculous tools, yet they place a computational layer between the photographer and the light.

When looking through a coincidence-type optical rangefinder mechanism, there is no digital interpolation or sensor lag. You peer through a pure optical prism beam-splitter. The yellow split-image patch moves across the field as your fingers rotate the brass helicoid of the lens until the two images snap into perfect alignment.

### Why Film Persists in My Workflow

1. **Deliberate Scarcity:** A 36-exposure roll demands that every shutter click be justified. You study the light, anticipate the gesture, and meter the shadows with an incident handheld meter.
2. **Dynamic Range in Highlights:** Silver halide grains possess a shoulder curve that gently rolls off high-key highlights rather than abruptly clipping to pure digital RGB white (`#FFFFFF`).
3. **The Tactile Feedback:** The damp mechanical snick of a cloth focal plane shutter releases zero vibrational shock, allowing sharp handheld shooting at speeds as low as 1/15th of a second.
